How the combat works

Frame data
Every move is startup, active and recovery frames at 60 per second. Only active frames can hit. All of it lives in the WEAPONS table, so balancing means editing numbers.
Hitstop
On contact both fighters freeze for 5 to 11 frames, longer for bigger hits. The target shakes, the screen shakes. This is most of the feeling of weight.
Input buffer
A button press is remembered for 8 frames, so the next hit of a combo comes out even when you press a little early.
Cancel windows
During a move's recovery, a buffered press switches to the move named in its next field. Those links are the combo chains.
Juggle decay
Each air hit pops the enemy a little less, and they bounce off the ground only once per juggle, so every combo ends eventually.
Attack tokens
Only two raiders may attack at the same time. The rest circle at a distance, which keeps crowds fair and readable.

Moving to Godot later: the move tables become Resource files, the state names become an enum in a match block, tick() becomes _physics_process at 60 Hz, and hitboxes become Area2D shapes switched on during active frames. The numbers carry over unchanged.
